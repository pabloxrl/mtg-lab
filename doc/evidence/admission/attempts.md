# Retained admission attempts

Archives contain privileged observations, including hidden cards and RNG state.
Extract them only into a private directory. Archive entries use directory mode
0700 and file mode 0600. Each adjacent JSON manifest records the archive hash and
every uncompressed file's SHA-256 and size. These are test artifacts, never a
policy-visible replay interface. The archives retain the original artifact bytes;
previous committed audit evidence is unchanged.

The generation archive contains the standard recorder's eight origins and eight
replays, plus both occurrence-aware generation versions. Standard recorder
origins did not witness pre-shuffle duplicate identities and are **unadmitted**.
The occurrence-aware generation binds copies at creation. Its later enriched
version adds observed effective characteristics for translation; captured states
are provenance, not authored expected results. The same eight deterministic
origins are repeated, not counted as new distinct games.

Diagnostic native and XMage archives retain the directed prefixes, full-game
adapter attempts and invalid-tape attempts. Their logs and first comparisons
retain the failed missing-damage-selection check, Thrill cost-order callback,
ability identity namespace, stale trigger-boundary observation and malformed
field rejection cursor. These were adapter/verifier defects; no engine output
was adopted as an expected checkpoint. The first XMage build exited with -9;
the unchanged warm retry passed. Diagnostic probes predating the complete runner
do not have complete admission provenance and remain unadmitted.

Candidate-v1 and both candidate-v2 archives contain complete runner receipts,
inputs, actual per-engine observations, invalid tapes, comparison results and
all eight compatibility-family runs. They passed their then-current verifier.
Subsequent negative controls exposed missing raw-identity and opening/pre-SBA
checks, so these receipts remain historical candidates, not final acceptance.
The recovered v1 source snapshot was checked file-for-file against its immutable
receipt hashes; v2 sources were copied and similarly hash-checked before edits.
Both reconstruction evidence and earlier first differences remain retained.

Candidate-v3 completed all engine and compatibility runs but preceded the
auxiliary nested-field repair. Its exact source snapshot and receipt are retained
separately; it is not final acceptance. The active torture pass was deliberately
interrupted after the gap was reproduced, and queued reference/lint commands
were canceled before executing. Their logs are retained. All required checks
restart on the repaired candidate.

Each final reference invocation has its own fresh privileged directory and
receipt. Repeated executions of the same frozen inputs never increase the
sixteen-game distinctness quota. Ordinary discovery also verifies these same
inputs; it does not generate additional candidate games. A failed, truncated,
unsupported, duplicate or disputed attempt cannot fill an admission row.
